## 1. How the films built troll characters
- Felt/flock skin: characters built like gummy bears flocked in velvet; world made of felt leaves and carpet ground. [S] https://www.cartoonbrew.com/cgi/trolls-director-mike-mitchell-no-mythology-thingsive-never-felt-free-144212.html ; https://postmagazine.com/Publications/Post-Magazine/2016/November-1-2016/-i-Trolls-i-.aspx (excerpts)
- Origin is the stubby troll doll with woolly bright hair, pushed toward a handmade-craft look. [S] https://www.npr.org/2016/11/05/500750170/beware-the-hair-trolls-hit-the-big-screen-and-bring-scrapbooks-with-them (excerpt)
- Fuzz/felt from displacement plus fur; World Tour extended it into a fabric library, mostly felt. [S] https://www.awn.com/animationworld/music-and-fiber-collide-melodiously-trolls-world-tour (excerpt)
- Proportions: bigger heads, shorter limbs, barely any elbows. [S] https://www.syfy.com/index.php/syfy-wire/what-is-trolls-band-together-about-the-filmmakers-explain-origin (fetched)
- Hair is the base motif; over half the silhouette; animators get hair rigs and guide curves, then character effects simulate. [S] https://research.dreamworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/26-0269-missey-Edited.pdf ; Post Magazine (excerpts)
- Colour: the art director looked for "opposites in shape, opposites in color" in a pairing. [S] https://www.cgw.com/Publications/CGW/2016/Volume-39-Issue-5-Sept-Oct-2016-/Making-Happy-Happen.aspx ; https://www.goldderby.com/video/tim-lamb-qa-trolls-art-director/ (excerpts)
- Pear/egg bodies, one dominant hue per skin, hair in a contrasting hue, one costume accent. [I]
- Small-screen read: fuzz reads as soft edge and sheen, not visible fibre; silhouette and flat hue carry it. [I] For us: stitch lines and rim sheen matter more than fibre detail.
- Not found: head/body ratios, colour scripts, hair-emote rules. Estimate [I]: head 45-50 % of height, legs under 25 %.

## 2. Contrasting pair in motion (thin sourcing)
- The grump "longs for a connection, but he won't allow himself to open up. But, he's more than a grump." [S] Hollywood Reporter behind-the-screen piece on the 2016 film (excerpt)
- Animators were given a loose, rough, fun movement style. [S] https://awn.com/animationworld/mike-mitchell-and-walt-dohrn-talk-trolls (excerpt)
- Principles [I]:
  - Optimist: wide stride, bounce each step, head and hair lead, big arm swing, quick starts, hair high and springy.
  - Cautious partner: shorter stride, flat footfall, lower head, shoulders in, hair held low, small delayed reaction after the lead moves.
  - Together: the cautious one trails by 0.3-0.6 s, matches speed with smaller steps, catches up with a tiny hop, stops slightly behind and to the side.
  - Warmth tell: one beat where the cautious one copies the lead's idle bounce at half amplitude.
  - Hair as mood: A's plume sways high and late; B's column is stiffer, droops when wary, perks when pleased.

## 3. Steer away from
- The two lead film characters: pink skin and pink hair with a flower/crown-style hair accent and scrapbook motif; a grey (formerly blue) troll with upright blue-grey hair, survival vest and bunker gear, scowl. [I], pink and grey facts [S] (character list pages, excerpts)
- Other film trolls a design could resemble [I]: full-body silver glitter; large round pale-blue body with a pet worm; tiny and very muscular; long giraffe neck; green skin with headphones; twins with joined hair; lavender skin with tall swept-up hair; punk spikes and studs; matching stage outfits.
- Generic traps: grey or blue-grey body, vest, hot-pink body, all-glitter surface, rainbow/ombre hair on B, spiky upright hair, crown or flower on the head, matching outfits, hug lines, "happy" slogans, film songs.

## 4. Troll B directions (palette-only; none uses grey, vest, glitter, ombre, crown or flower)
1. **Butter Drizzle**: butter #F7DC6F short wide bean body; violet #7A52C7 fat rope-twist hair column, tip flops forward; sky #7EC8E8 knit poncho; small folded orange umbrella; slow flat-footed trudge, checks the sky.
2. **Lime Sprout**: lime #A7D84A tall narrow egg; lavender #B79AE0 straight blunt-topped column; orange striped sweater with butter bands; tiny butter teacup in both hands; careful short steps. Easiest for Tripo per the subagent.
3. **Sky Pebble**: sky #7EC8E8 low pebble; tomato tuft that droops; lavender turtleneck; lime magnifier. Nearest the grey-blue trap.

## Decision (driver, 2026-11-22)
Direction 1, modified. Lime Sprout would put a lime troll next to mint troll A (two greens) and break the one sourced pairing rule (opposites in colour and shape). B is the warm-body/cool-hair inverse of A:
- Body: butter #F7DC6F felt, short wide bean (A is a taller pear), big head, barely-there elbows.
- Hair: violet #7A52C7 single-hue rope-twist column, tip flopping slightly forward; straight enough for a 4-bone chain.
- Costume: sky #7EC8E8 knit sweater with a rolled collar (no poncho: it fuses with the body in image-to-3D).
- Prop: a closed orange #F59A3C umbrella strapped diagonally across the back (always ready for rain); nothing in the hands, so the arms rig cleanly.
- Personality: cautious, dry, warm underneath. Walk shorter and flatter than A; hair held lower and stiffer.

## 5. Image-to-3D input (Tripo)
- Clear, centred subject, clean background, even diffuse light, no baked shadows or highlights. [S] Tripo blog/tutorial pages (excerpts)
- PNG/JPEG under 10 MB. [S]
- Multi-view orthographic sheets limit guessing about the back; switch to multi-view if a single image fails. [S] https://www.tripo3d.ai/getting-started/improve-ai-3d-model-quality-tips-results (excerpt)
- Our recommendation [I]: neutral A-pose, arms slightly away from the torso, feet apart; same scale across views on flat white; hair a straight column clear of the collar; prop clear of the arms; flat felt hues, stitches as simple darker lines.
