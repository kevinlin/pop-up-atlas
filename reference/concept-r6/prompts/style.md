Photograph of a real handmade tabletop diorama, shot on a full-frame camera with a tilt-shift lens: camera about 35 degrees above the table, a wide horizontal band of sharp focus through the middle 40 percent of the frame, smooth gentle blur toward the top and bottom edges, slightly boosted saturation like miniature photography.

The diorama is an open scrapbook made of felt and paper. Its pages are cream cardstock with visible fibre and torn white-cored edges, felt borders with contrasting blanket stitch, a few skewed strips of semi-translucent washi tape. Ground appliqué: large overlapping felt shapes layered two or three deep, scattered French knots, a few sequins and tiny sewn-on buttons. Out of the flat pages rise pop-up pieces built like a real pop-up book: layered cut-paper and felt planes on v-folds and parallel folds, mushroom caps and cupcake-tree crowns built as rounded domes from several curved cards, visible glue tabs and crease lines, soft warm contact shadows darkest at each hinge, 1-2 mm craft felt with a fuzzy fibre halo that glows warm at the silhouette edges, running-stitch seams with slight puckering, fine hex glitter cardstock on edges that throws sparse, very bright pinpoint sparkles. Each grove keeps to one colour family.

Palette: saturated candy rainbow felt - hot pink E8478F, pink F4A6C8, lavender B79AE0, violet 7A52C7, sky 7EC8E8, mint 9EE0B8, lime A7D84A, butter yellow F7DC6F, orange F59A3C, tomato D9453B - on cream F6EAD2 pages.

Lighting: warm low sunlight key from the upper left, a distinct pink-violet fill from the right side, soft shadows between paper layers, a warm fuzz rim on the felt creatures.

Two tiny original felt creatures, about the height of a thimble, adorable and cute, soft and round like plush toys, each with one soft rounded head-body shape, a big head, big glossy black button eyes with a white highlight dot set wide and low, rosy cheeks, a small stitched mouth, stubby felt arms and legs, and tall upswept fluffy yarn hair.
