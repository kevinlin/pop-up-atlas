# Research: real craft traits

From a web research sub-agent (2026-11-20), checked by the driver. Marked *(est)* = craft-knowledge estimate, not sourced.

## Pop-up books
- Constructions: v-fold (stage-like set at an angle from the spine), parallel fold (flat card lifted by two hinged tabs), stepped layered planes. *(est)*
- Tunnel/layered books combine cut-out layers into one scene ([Wikipedia: Pop-up book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop-up_book)).
- Cardstock 176-298 gsm ≈ 0.2-0.35 mm ([Vistaprint](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/paper-weight-chart), [JukeBox](https://www.jukeboxprint.com/blog/paper-weight-demystified-pound-points-grams)). Pop-up parts 0.2-0.4 mm, base pages 1-2 mm board. *(est)*
- Cut edge reads as a thin lighter line; contact shadows darkest at the hinge, soft and warm, falling off within 1-2 layer depths; glue bleed, white crease cracks, slight warping. *(est)*

## Felt
- Craft felt 1-1.5 mm; wool blend 1.0-1.2 mm ([retailer guide](https://stationery.lazada.com.ph/how-to-choose-the-right-felt-paper-for-sewing-and-craft-projects-ph/)).
- Almost purely diffuse; fuzzy bright rim at grazing angles (0.5-2 mm fibre halo); no clean silhouette; shadows take the felt's hue. Shader: sheen / Fresnel rim, no specular lobe. *(est)*
- Blanket stitch spacing 3-6 mm, contrasting 2-3 strand floss sitting proud, slight pucker between stitches. *(est)*
- Candy craft-felt palette *(est)*: `#F4A6C8` `#E8478F` `#B79AE0` `#7A52C7` `#7EC8E8` `#9EE0B8` `#A7D84A` `#F7DC6F` `#F59A3C` `#F6EAD2` `#D9453B`.

## Scrapbook
- Cardstock textures: smooth, linen, canvas, kraft `#B38B5C` *(est)*. Torn edge shows a white fibrous core 1-3 mm wide. *(est)*
- Washi tape ~15 mm, semi-translucent, matte, often skewed, faint hairline shadow ([Altenew](https://altenew.com/blogs/paper-crafting-inspiration-and-tips/how-to-use-washi-tape-on-your-scrapbook-layouts)).
- Layers rotated 1-5°, each adding a hairline shadow. *(est)*

## Glitter
- Hex flake sizes: 1/128" ≈ 0.2 mm, 1/64" ≈ 0.4 mm, 1/40" ≈ 0.5-0.6 mm ([Meadowbrook](https://meadowbrookglitter.com/glitter-size-chart/), [ArtGlitter](https://www.artglitter.com/blogs/how-to/what-are-the-different-glitter-sizes)).
- Each flake is a tiny randomly angled mirror: sparse, very bright pixel-scale hits that flicker with view/light movement; dim saturated base between hits; hard cut-off, no soft glow. Shader: hashed per-flake normals + sharp specular threshold. *(est)*

## Tilt-shift
- High angle, "from above but not directly above" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/how-to/photography-video-capture/how-to-create-a-tilt-shift-effect-1311500)); 35-50° down *(est)*.
- Thin wedge of focus ([Wikipedia: Tilt-shift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tilt%E2%80%93shift_photography)). Sharp band 20-35 % of frame height, falloff over 20-25 % each side, max blur ≈ 1-2 % of image width, saturation +20-40 % *(est)*.

## Gaps
No openly licensed photo of a full felt-and-paper diorama, a modern washi scrapbook page, or glitter cardstock was found. Tilt-shift numbers and the palette are estimates.
