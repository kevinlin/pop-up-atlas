# Round 4 forest style

The forest is a handmade pop-up stage on an open cream-cardstock scrapbook spread on a warm wood table. Use a downward camera pitch of about 45° and a tilt-shift focus band through the middle third. Keep the troll and the main action sharp. Blur the near edge and let the far edge recede into brighter, softer, less saturated haze. A stitched border, layered paper edge or shallow gutter keeps the book legible in every shot.

The original walker has a pear-shaped mint needle-felt body, sewn black button eyes, a peach felt nose, stubby limbs, a mustard knitted scarf and a tomato-red satchel. One tall swept bundle of yarn runs from orange roots through hot pink to violet tips. The round-3 turntable anchors its shape and colors. Earlier concepts guide the tabletop photography and handcraft, and round-3 gameplay grounds the character and motion.

Use matte, visibly fibrous felt and cut cardstock, contrasting stitch floss, yarn cords, and paper hinges that cast contact shadows. Felt moss in groves ranges from tight fuzz to loose tufts. Let cream cardstock open up in clearings and show through at edges and gutters. Use glitter as isolated pinpoints, especially on dark surfaces.

Give each stage one dominant hue family. Preserve the troll's small costume colors and the book's cream and wood cues. Backlight the felt from the far side of the spread, dapple the ground and cluster soft bokeh in the far band. Keep airborne fuzz small enough to distinguish it from bokeh and glitter. Lanterns, flower cores and house windows emit localized light. Where the walker triggers a fold, show a short bright flash and a few crisp particles alongside softer settled emitters.

All plants, houses and props have original, faceless designs. Keep the image free of lettering and marks. The [round-4 contract](../../docs/contracts.md#look-round-4-magical-forest-stage) defines the quantitative gameplay targets. The [research exclusions](../../docs/research/trolls-stage-design.md#do-not-copy) apply to the entire set.
