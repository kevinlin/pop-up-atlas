**Three recurring fixes**

1. Clear the troll’s silhouette and keep the book’s front edge or gutter in frame.
2. Use warm directional light, with haze and small glows placed behind the walking lane to build depth.
3. Make the construction visible through continuous felt surfaces, folded cardstock supports, and layered page edges.

**Lessons learned**  
Check silhouette and framing at thumbnail size. Check fibers, folds, and light spill at full size.
