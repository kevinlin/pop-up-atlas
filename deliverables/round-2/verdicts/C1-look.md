| 1 | Compose each shot around one readable action. Enlarge the flanking scenery in the wide shot and clear the traveller’s face, bag, and feet in close-ups. | The viewer sees a traveller crossing a bridge or following a path at a glance. The forest has a scale relative to the figure. |

**Lesson learned:** A single lit paper brace or a scarf loop you can trace does more to sell the handmade illusion than another blurred row of scenery.
